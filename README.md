## featured projects

### 🧾 Quanty Management

electronic invoicing and business management system designed for businesses in El Salvador.

the system is being developed to manage the complete sales workflow while integrating electronic tax documents and administrative tools.

**status:** 🚧 in development

**main features:**
- electronic invoicing
- DTE generation and management
- inventory management
- sales management
- customers and suppliers
- user roles and permissions
- reports
- petty cash management
- payment workflows

**stack:**
- react + vite
- node.js + express
- postgresql
- tailwind css

**status:** 🚧 in development

- student profiles

- parental dashboard
- parental PIN protection

- child-friendly interface

- postgresql

### 🎓 Colegio La Fe — Admission Exam System

web-based admission examination system developed for **Colegio La Fe**.

the platform digitalizes the admission process, allowing prospective students to complete evaluations online while giving the institution tools to manage applicants and results.

**status:** ✅ completed

**live:**  
https://admisionesclf.website

**main features:**
- online admission exams
- applicant registration
- question management
- automatic evaluation
- result management
- institutional administration
